  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">函数定义：int Findmin(int b[],int n)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">要求b是一维数组，所以调用时必须给数组名：Findmin(b,5)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">而不是给数组元素：Findmin(b[5],5)，而且数组元素b[5]并不存在。</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp; 可以使用5*5矩阵的每一行为一个一维数组来调用：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp; c[m]=Findmin(a[m],5);</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">不必使用b数组。</span></p>
