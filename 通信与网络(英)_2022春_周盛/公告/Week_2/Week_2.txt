内容: This week, we have gone through the main elements of information theory.
Kraft's inequality, the necessary condition for uniquely decodable code, maps the codewords to non-overlapping segments, which leads to the lower and upper bounds for optimal prefix codes. As a specific example, Huffman coding constructs an optimal prefix code for the discrete memoryless channels. 
Then we studied joint entropy and conditional entropy, with independence and deterministic function as two extreme cases. Mutual information is the information-gain of Y from X's knowledge and vice versa. The maximum mutual information between channel inputs and outputs is the channel capacity. We verified the capacity for BSC is achieved when input is equiprobable.
Finally, differential entropy, which represents relative uncertainty, addresses continuous random variables.
