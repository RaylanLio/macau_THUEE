内容: Welcome aboard!
This week we have gone through an overview of the digital communication system, in which we emphasize two steps. The first is to encode the source symbol to bits efficiently, and the second is to transmit the bits robustly against noise.
The first chapter, information theory, is about the first step. We characterize the amount of information of an event / an r.v. using entropy, which is the lower bound of average codeword length for lossless compression. With the properties to follow, the mathematical expression of entropy is uniquely determined.
For a better understanding of digital communication systems and networks, please read Haykin - Digital Communication Systems 1.1-1.5. More concrete examples can be found in *Forouzan - Data Communications and Network Chap 1-2. (Reference with * is optional)
Please refer to Haykin - Digital Communication Systems 5.1-5.3 for the information theory part. If you have trouble with the probability theory or need a quick comeback,  *Digital Communication Systems 3.1-3.6 is recommended.
