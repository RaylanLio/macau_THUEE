内容: Today we learned the rest of Quantization and Pulse Code Modulation (PCM). 
Lloyd-Max conditions are the necessary and sufficient conditions for a quantizer to be optimal. There are also non-uniform quantizer designs that are widely used in real-world systems, such as 13-piece A-law quantizer.
The last part, encoding, turns a discrete value into a bit sequence. So far, analog signals are transformed to bit sequences with specific bit-rate, e.g. 64kbps for voice, 1411kbps for Audio CD.
One of the most important parts of the semester, baseband modulation, opens this week.
Relationship: Bit sequence -> Symbol sequence -> Waveforms
