  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">void paixu(char **q,int n)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">q是指向指针的指针，这里是指针数组，那么q[j]或写为*(q+j)是指向字符串的指针，strcmp是要比较两个字符串的大小，写为： if(strcmp(**(q+j),**(q+j+1))==-1)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">是错误的，**(q+j)和**(q+j+1)第j和j+1个字符串的第一个字符，应改为：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">if(strcmp(*(q+j),*(q+j+1))==-1)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">或者： &nbsp;&nbsp;if(strcmp(q[j],q[j+1])==-1)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify">&nbsp;</p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">另一个问题是，当循环到j=n-1时，*(q+j+1)将数组越界，造成致命错误。&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; </span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">for(j=i;j&lt;n;j=j+1) 应改为：&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; for(j=i;j&lt;n-1;j=j+1)</span></p>
