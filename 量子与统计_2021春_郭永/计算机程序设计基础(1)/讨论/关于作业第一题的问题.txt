  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">如果输出在double数之间加一逗号，fprintf(fp, "%lf,", a[i]); 那么就把每个double分割开了，读入时在输入格式double数后也加一逗号，就能正确读入每一个double数。而如果用</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">fprintf(fp, "%lf", a[i]);</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">将会导致输出到文件中的数据是你打开文件所看到的，首位相连：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">1.0000002.0000003.00000044.0000006.000000……..</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">这样当用fscanf(fp, "%lf", &amp;b); 读入时，每次遇到第二个小数点才停止，读入的实际上是：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">1.0000002&nbsp; .0000003&nbsp; .00000044&nbsp; .0000006&nbsp; .0000006 ……..</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">因为输出是6位小数，四舍五入，所以结果是：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">1.000000&nbsp; 0.000000&nbsp; 0.000000&nbsp; 0.000001&nbsp; 0.000000 ……..</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">因此输出到文件中的数据必须要有格式控制，两个double数之间应该有空格或者回车。</span></p>
