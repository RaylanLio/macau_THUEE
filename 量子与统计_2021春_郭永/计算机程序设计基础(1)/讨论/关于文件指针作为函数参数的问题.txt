  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">if((fp=fopen("stu.dat","w+b"))==NULL)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">打开方式为"w+b"，是写读方式，为了写打开一个文件，如果文件已经存在，文件原有的内容会被清除，因此前面生成的stu.dat文件内容已经丢失，fread未读入数据，暑促的额是未初始化的值-842150451。应改为：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">if((fp=fopen("stu.dat","r+b"))==NULL)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">或</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">if((fp=fopen("stu.dat","rb"))==NULL)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">如果已经读入文件尾，不用fseek(fp,0L,SEEK_SET); 把文件指针绕回到文件开始处，</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">fread(b,sizeof(struct hh),3,fp);</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">也将无法正确读入数据，输出0，0，0</span></p>
