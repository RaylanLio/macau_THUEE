  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">VS2008上编译结果为：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">test.c(19) : warning C4715: “F”: 不是所有的控件路径都返回值</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">因为你的程序当 if (n == 1 &amp;&amp; n == 2) 不成立时，没有返回值。</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">运行结果是：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">请输入n1</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">-858993460请按任意键继续. . .</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify">&nbsp;</p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">n为1时结果为随机数，也就是没有返回值。因为if (n == 1 &amp;&amp; n == 2) 中n任何时候都不可能既等于1又等于2。</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">应改为：if (n == 1 || n == 2)</span></p>
