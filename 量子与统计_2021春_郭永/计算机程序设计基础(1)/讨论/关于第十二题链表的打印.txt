  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">你定义：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">struct stunode</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">{</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; struct student* data;</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; struct stunode* next;</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">};</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">那么data在结构体stunode中只是一个指针，</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; p = (struct stunode*)malloc(sizeof(struct stunode));</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">p所申请的动态内存块中只有两个指针data和next，此时</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; p-&gt;data-&gt;num = stu[n].num; p-&gt;data-&gt;grade = stu[n].grade;</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; for (k = 0; k &lt; 10; k++)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; {</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; p-&gt;data-&gt;name[k] = stu[n].name[k];</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; }</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">会引起致命错误，除非再用malloc为data申请一块struct student大小的内存。</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">你的结构体定义应改为：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">struct stunode</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">{</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; struct student &nbsp;data;</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; struct stunode* next;</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">};</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">因为data是结构体，程序中，所有 p-&gt;data-&gt; 都应改为：p-&gt;data.</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">上面的代码应改为：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; p-&gt;data.num = stu[n].num; p-&gt;data.grade = stu[n].grade;</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; strcpy(p-&gt;data.name, stu[n].name);</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">字符串复制用strcpy函数。</span></p>
