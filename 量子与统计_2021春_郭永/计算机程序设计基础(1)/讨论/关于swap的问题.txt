  <p><span style="font-size:10.5pt">t</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">是指针，</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">int *t=p; </span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">通过赋初值让</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">t</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">指向了</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">p</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">，</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">*t=*p;</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">相当于</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">p</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">给自己赋值，没有改变所指单元的值。但</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">*p=*q; </span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">让</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">p</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">所指单元</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">a</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">赋值为</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">q</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">所指单元</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">b</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">的值</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">3</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">，</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">*q=*t; </span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">等价于</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">*q=*p;</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">，所以</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">q</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">所指单元内容还是</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">3</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">，因此最后输出结果是</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">3</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">，</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">3</span></p>
