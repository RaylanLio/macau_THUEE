  <p>p=(struct people*)malloc(sizeof(struct people*));</p> 
  <p>申请的是struct people结构体而不是struct people结构体指针，应改为：</p> 
  <p>&nbsp;p=(struct people*)malloc(sizeof(struct people));</p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">否则会造成每个结点的动态内存越界，free(p)无法正常释放产生致命错误。</span></p>
