  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">你只要把析构函数：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; virtual ~Queue() {delete []Front;}</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">中的delete语句去掉，程序运行就不会出现致命错误，所以是释放动态内存引起的致命错误。</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;无论是C还是C++的动态内存，用Front=new T[max=size]; 申请后动态内存块后，释放时Front指针必须指向这块动态内存的首地址，如果进行过Front++; 改变了Front指针的值，delete []Front; 将无法正常释放，引起致命错误。</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;你程序的问题在于Out函数中return *Front++; 修改了Front。</span></p> 
  <p><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;为什么队列空要修改</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">Front</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">呢？而且队列里用</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">top</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">来指示什么？队列有队头指针</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">Front</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">和队尾指针</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">Tail</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">，当</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">Tail==Front</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">时表示队列空，当</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">Tail==Front+max</span><span style="font-size:10.5pt">时表示队列满，足以指示队列状态。</span></p>
