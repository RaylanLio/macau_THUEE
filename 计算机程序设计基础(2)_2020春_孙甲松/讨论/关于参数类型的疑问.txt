  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">如果把=操作符成员函数的定义改为：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp; &nbsp; String&amp;String::operator=(String &amp;s3)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">那么应该同时把String类中=操作符成员函数声明改为：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; String&amp;operator =(String &amp;s2);</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">使得两者保持一致，都是引用方式，这样应该就不会出现你说的编译错误。</span></p>

  <p>&nbsp; &nbsp; String&amp;String::operator=(const String &amp;s3)</p>

  <p>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; String&amp;operator =(const String &amp;s2);</p> 
  <p>使得两者保持一致，都是常引用方式。</p>
