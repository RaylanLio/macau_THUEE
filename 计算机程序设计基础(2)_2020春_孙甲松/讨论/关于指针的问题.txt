  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">你的程序运行出现致命错误的原因是：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp; for(i=0;i&lt;n;i++)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; {</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;p[i]=new char[80];</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;p[i]="";</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp; &nbsp; }</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">在为指针p[i]申请了一块长度为80个字节的动态内存后，又让p[i]指针指向了一个空字符串，空字符串是常量字符串，不能修改，所以执行后面的语句：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; strcpy(p[k++],temp); </span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">会提示写入位置发生访问冲突，引起致命错误。把 p[i]=""; 语句直接注释掉就好了。</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">如果想把每个p[i]所指的动态内存初始化为空字符串，应该改为:</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;strcpy(p[i], "");</span></p>
