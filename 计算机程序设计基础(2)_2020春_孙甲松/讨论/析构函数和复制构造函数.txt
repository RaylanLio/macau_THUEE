  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">如果把Rectangle的构造函数改为：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">Rectangle::Rectangle(Point p1, Point p2)</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">{</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; pright=Point(p1.x,p1.y);</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; pleft = Point(p2.x,p2.y);</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">}</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">会产生编译错误：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">error C2512: “Point”: 没有合适的默认构造函数可用</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">因为Rectangle构造函数执行时，会先调用缺省值的Point构造函数构造内嵌对象pright, pleft，但你的Point构造函数没有缺省值，所以产生编译错误。把Point类中的构造函数说明改为：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Point(double x1=0, double y1=0);</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">就可以解决这个问题。</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">你的程序只打印Point析构信息，不打印Point构造和复制构造信息，就会造成一种错觉，程序刚开始就调用了4次析构函数，你把Point构造函数和复制构造函数中都加入打印信息：</span></p> 
  <p style="margin-left:0cm; margin-right:0cm; text-align:justify"><span style="font-size:10.5pt">Point::Point(double x1, double y1) :x (x1), y (y1){cout &lt;&lt; "Point Constructor called！" &lt;&lt; endl; }</span></p>
